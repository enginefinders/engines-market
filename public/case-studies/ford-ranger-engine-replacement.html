Early warning

The ticking noise was not a tappet. It was low oil pressure.

The danger signal was not low oil level. It was the pump losing the ability to maintain pressure, especially at low RPM and when hot.

MondayFaint ticking at idle

Audible when cold, quieter when warm, but never fully gone.

ThursdayOil light flickered

The dipstick showed full oil, so the vehicle was driven again.

FridaySolid oil light, knock, silence

The engine seized within minutes on the M6.

If your 3.2 Duratorq ticks at idle or the oil pressure light flickers even once, stop driving and test oil pressure. A £50-£100 test can confirm pump failure before the bottom end seizes.
Failure timeline

5,000 miles was not the story - five days was.

From first tick to seizure took less than a week. The owner assumed diesel injector or tappet noise because the oil level looked normal.

What the owner assumed

"Probably a tappet or injector." Common diesel noise was assumed, not oil pressure failure.

What was actually happening

The oil pump was failing internally. Bearings starved, the crankshaft scored, and the bottom end seized.

The uncertainty play

The engine was replaced, but the turbo had also run without oil.

When the oil pump fails, every component relying on oil pressure is at risk. The turbo could fail weeks or months after the replacement engine is fitted.

Path A - chosen

Fit only the used engine and risk the existing turbo.

£3,800

If turbo fails later: add £800-£1,200 plus labour. Kept upfront cost down but left a known risk.

Path B

Replace the turbo preventatively at the same time as the engine.

£4,500

£3,800 engine + ~£700 turbo. Eliminates the risk entirely.

Lessons learned

An oil pressure light is not a suggestion. It is a stop-driving-now warning.

If your 3.2 Duratorq Ranger makes a ticking noise at idle or the oil pressure light flickers even once, stop driving and test the oil pressure before the bottom end seizes.

Oil pressure test£50-£100

Can confirm whether the pump is losing pressure before major damage occurs.

Preventative oil pump£500-£1,000

Keeps the engine running safely if the issue is caught early.

After seizure - used engine£3,500-£4,500

Car off the road for 5-7 days and short warranty.

Reconditioned engine£4,500-£6,000+

Higher cost, but new oil pump and stronger warranty.

A £600 oil pump replacement could have avoided the £3,800 used-engine replacement. An £80 oil pressure test at the first flicker could have flagged the failure even earlier.
