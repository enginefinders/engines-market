The death rattle

The sound that told the owner to stop driving immediately.

The owner first noticed a brief rattling noise on cold starts around 85,000 miles. The noise lasted approximately two seconds after ignition, then disappeared. The car drove normally otherwise - no loss of power, no warning lights.

What they thought"Maybe it's just the engine settling."

Oil level was fine. No warning lights. Car drove normally.

What it actually wasSpring-loaded tensioner failing

The 2.0 TFSI (EA888 Gen2) is equipped with a timing chain tensioner that uses a spring-loaded design rather than oil pressure alone. According to workshop consensus [INDUSTRY ESTIMATE], the original tensioner design can fail without warning.
