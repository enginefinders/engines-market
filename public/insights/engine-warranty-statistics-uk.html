Frequently asked questions

Common questions about engine warranties

What is the average engine warranty claim cost in the UK? +
The average engine-related dispute claim brought to The Motor Ombudsman is £2,755 [THIRD-PARTY]. For drivetrain-related disputes, the average rises to £2,953 [THIRD-PARTY]. However, individual claims can be much higher - the highest recorded engine warranty claim in the UK was £26,233 for a 2016 BMW [THIRD-PARTY].
Which car brand has the lowest warranty claim rate? +
Lexus has the lowest claim rate at 5.79% [THIRD-PARTY: Warranty Group, 2025]. Toyota follows at 8.78%, and Suzuki at 10.80%. This reflects the broader reputation of Japanese brands for reliability.
What is the most common engine warranty claim? +
According to RAC Warranty data, the most common claim items are electrical systems (most frequent), followed by engine (costliest and most common cause), front suspension, and cooling system [THIRD-PARTY].
How much does a reconditioned engine warranty cover? +
Reconditioned engines typically come with a 12-24 month warranty covering parts and labour [EM-OBSERVED]. This is significantly better than used engines, which usually offer only 1-3 months of supply-only coverage. The warranty premium for reconditioned engines (typically £600-£1,000 more than used) provides significant protection.
Are hybrid engines covered by warranty? +
Yes, hybrid engines are covered by warranty, but claims are rising sharply. Hybrid claims increased from 1.5% to 3.1% of all claims in one year - a 107% increase [THIRD-PARTY]. This reflects the growing hybrid vehicle parc and the unique complexity of hybrid powertrains.
What was the largest engine warranty claim in the UK? +
The largest recorded engine warranty claim in the UK was £26,233 for a 2016 BMW (RAC Warranty, 2024) [THIRD-PARTY]. Other high-value claims include a Jaguar F-Pace SVR at £23,000 (WSG, 2025) and an Audi (2020) at £22,954 (RAC Warranty, 2025).
Is an extended warranty worth it for a premium car? +
Yes. The top 5 highest warranty claims in the UK all exceeded £19,000, with the highest at £26,233 [THIRD-PARTY]. Without extended warranty, owners of premium vehicles like the Jaguar F-Pace SVR, BMW, Audi, and Range Rover Sport would have faced catastrophic financial losses. The data strongly supports purchasing extended warranty for premium vehicles.
What is the satisfaction rate for reconditioned engine warranties? +
Post-sale surveys conducted by Engines Market show a 92% satisfaction rate among reconditioned engine customers [EM-SURVEY]. This includes warranty-related outcomes, with most claims being resolved without dispute. The survey was conducted 3-6 months after installation with a sample size of 100+ customers.
