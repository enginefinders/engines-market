What is engine knock — and why is it critical?

📖

What is engine knock?

Engine knock (also known as detonation) is the abnormal combustion of air-fuel mixture in the cylinders, causing a metallic knocking sound and potentially serious engine damage.

⚠️

Why it matters

If left unresolved, engine knock can lead to piston, bearing and cylinder damage, resulting in extremely high repair costs or even complete engine failure.

⚙️
KEY TAKEAWAY

Engine knock is a warning sign. The sooner you identify and resolve the cause, the more likely you can avoid major engine damage and costly repairs.

Engine Knock Symptoms

  • Rattling on startup
  • Louder under acceleration
  • More noticeable when hot

  • Reduced fuel economy
  • Check engine light (often)
⚠️ Warning Signs

Audio Descriptions — What to Listen For

Frequently Asked Questions

How serious is engine knock?
Engine knock is very serious. If it's mechanical (rod knock), continuing to drive can destroy the block. Immediate inspection is recommended.
Can I still drive with engine knock?
Detonation knock allows gentle driving to a mechanic. If you hear a heavy rhythmic thudding that rises with RPM, turn off the engine immediately and arrange towing.
How much does it cost to fix engine knock?
Simple decarbonisation or fuel issues range from £150-£400. Bearing replacements cost £800-£1,500, while complete engine replacements range from £2,500 upwards.
What causes engine knock?
The primary causes are worn connecting rod bearings, low oil pressure, pre-ignition from incorrect fuel octane, or damaged timing components.
Can engine knock repair itself?
No. Mechanical bearing damage is irreversible. Ignoring it will inevitably lead to total crankshaft and engine failure.
How can I prevent engine knock?
Stick strictly to oil change intervals, check oil levels weekly, use high-grade manufacturer-specified fuel, and do not over-rev cold engines.
