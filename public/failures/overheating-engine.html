STOP DRIVING IMMEDIATELY — THIS IS CRITICAL
If your temperature gauge enters the red zone or you notice steam escaping from the bonnet: Pull over safely immediately. Turn off the engine—do not let it idle. Never open the expansion tank cap while the engine is hot due to the risk of severe steam scalding. Wait 30–60 minutes before inspecting coolant levels.

Cost & Risk Trajectory

How Fast Overheating Damage Escalates

Click any stage below to inspect the mechanical failure mechanism, immediate recommended actions, and repair cost ranges.

Likely Root Cause

Low coolant level from a minor radiator pinhole, hose weep, expansion tank cap seal, or thermostat sticking partially closed. Early warning phase.

Immediate Recommended Action

Pull over safely and check expansion tank level when completely cold. Top up with correct specification coolant mix and book a cooling system pressure test.

Estimated Repair Bill

£50–£200 for coolant top-up, radiator hose replacement, or thermostat replacement. Typical labour: 1–3 hours.

Likely Root Cause

Water pump impeller failure (plastic blades spinning freely), cooling fan motor or relay burnt out, or complete radiator core internal silt blockage.

Immediate Recommended Action

Stop driving immediately. Turn off engine and arrange flatbed recovery. Do not attempt to drive further—head gasket thermal failure is imminent.

Estimated Repair Bill

£200–£600 for water pump, cooling radiator, or electric fan assembly replacement. Typical labour: 2–5 hours.

Likely Root Cause

Blown cylinder head gasket. Combustion gases are breaching the cooling jacket fire rings, pressurising the cooling system and blowing coolant out the cap.

Immediate Recommended Action

Stop driving and call recovery. A professional block sniffer test is required to verify combustion gas presence before cylinder head removal and skimming.

Estimated Repair Bill

£600–£1,200 for cylinder head gasket replacement, head pressure testing, surface skim, and new head bolts. Labour: 8–15 hours.

Likely Root Cause

Cylinder head severely warped beyond maximum skimming tolerances or micro-cracked between exhaust valve seats from extreme thermal shock.

Immediate Recommended Action

Full mechanical teardown. Cylinder head replacement or precision reconditioning required. Compare total job estimate against vehicle market value.

Estimated Repair Bill

£1,200–£3,500 for replacement cylinder head assembly. If the engine block deck is warped: £2,500–£6,500+ for engine replacement.

Likely Root Cause

Complete engine seizure. Extreme heat expands aluminium pistons until they weld to cast iron/aluminium cylinder liners, shearing crank bearings.

Immediate Recommended Action

Do not attempt to jump-start or crank. Complete reconditioned engine supply and installation is the only mechanical solution remaining.

Estimated Repair Bill

£2,500–£6,500+ for a reconditioned engine replacement with warranty. On lower-value vehicles, this represents an immediate insurance/economic write-off.

Systematic Triage

7-Step Diagnostic Process: Finding the Root Cause

A competent specialist will never replace a head gasket without systematically identifying why the engine overheated first.

Step Diagnostic Check / Procedure What Garage Identifies Typical Garage Fee
1 Coolant Level & Expansion Tank Inspection Checks for low fluid level, discoloured coolant, or air locks. £10 – £30
2 Pressurised Leak Detection Test Pressurises cooling circuit to 1.5 bar to locate weeping hoses or radiator core leaks. £50 – £200
3 Thermostat Opening Verification Measures top/bottom radiator hose temperature differential using infrared thermometer. £95 – £215 (fitted)
4 Electric Cooling Fan Circuit Activation Commands high-speed fan relay via OBD2 scan tool and tests A/C fan cycling. £150 – £450
5 Water Pump Flow & Weep Hole Test Verifies coolant return flow rate into expansion bottle and checks pump shaft play. £115 – £700
6 Chemical Block Sniffer Test Draws expansion tank air through chemical reagent; blue turns yellow if CO2 exhaust gas is present. £50 – £150
7 Cylinder Leak-Down & Compression Test Quantifies mechanical cylinder pressure loss directly into adjacent coolant jackets. £100 – £200

If you are replacing the timing belt or timing chain, always replace both the water pump and thermostat simultaneously—the labour overlap saves hundreds of pounds.

Decision Framework

Repair vs Replace: What Should You Do?

Evaluate economic repair feasibility based on mechanical damage depth and vehicle residual market value.

Damage Severity Tier Recommended Repair Option Estimated Cost Economic Threshold / Decision Logic
Low Coolant / Minor Leak Hose replacement, expansion tank cap, top-up £50 – £200 Always economical—simple routine maintenance.
Stuck Thermostat Thermostat housing and seal replacement £95 – £215 Always replace immediately to prevent catastrophic warping.
Failed Water Pump Water pump replacement + fresh coolant flush £115 – £700 Critical priority; upgrade plastic impellers to metal.
Failed Cooling Fan Assembly Electric fan motor, resistor pack, or relay £150 – £450 Mandatory if fan does not spin when warm or A/C is activated.
Blown Head Gasket (Flat Head) Gasket replacement + light cylinder head skim £550 – £1,200 Viable on vehicles with market value exceeding £2,000–£3,000.
Warped / Cracked Cylinder Head Reconditioned or new cylinder head assembly £1,200 – £3,500 Viable on vehicles worth £4,000+; otherwise consider recon engine.
Complete Engine Seizure Reconditioned engine replacement £2,500 – £6,500+ Viable on vehicles worth £6,000+; otherwise salvage vehicle.
