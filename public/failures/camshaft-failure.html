Camshaft Failure UK Symptoms, Replacement Cost & Prevention

Camshaft failure is often caused by oil starvation. A worn cam lobe destroys the valve train. On interference engines, incorrect valve timing from a failed cam lobe can cause pistons to hit valves — turning a £400-£1,800 repair into a £2,500-£6,500 engine replacement. Regular oil changes are cheap insurance.

Important: Don't ignore early warning signs

Camshaft issues can quickly escalate into serious engine damage, leading to significantly higher repair costs. If you notice unusual noises, loss of power or warning lights, get your vehicle diagnosed by a qualified specialist as soon as possible. On interference engines, a cam/crank correlation code (P0016-P0019) is an emergency. If timing is off enough, pistons will hit valves. Don't clear the code and hope - diagnose immediately. A £400-£900 camshaft replacement now saves a £2,500-£6,500 engine replacement later.

Signs of camshaft failure

Don't ignore these warning signs

Early detection can save you thousands. If you notice any of the following symptoms, have your vehicle inspected by a specialist.

⚠️

Common Early Signs (Book diagnostic soon)

  • Tapping or rattling noise from the engine
  • Rough running or misfires
  • Loss of power or poor performance
  • Engine management light (EML) - P0016-P0019 (cam/crank correlation)
  • Unusual vibrations
  • Increased fuel consumption
🚨

Serious Warning Signs (Book diagnostic immediately)

  • Persistent loud ticking or knocking
  • Significant loss of power
  • Engine entering limp mode
  • Metal particles in engine oil (visible glitter during oil change)
  • Complete engine failure
  • Inability to start the vehicle

Unusual noises from the camshaft can be one of the first signs of serious engine problems.

Common causes of camshaft failure

Why can camshaft failure occur?

Camshaft failures can happen for a variety of reasons. Here are the most common causes.

01

Poor lubrication

Low oil levels, incorrect oil grade or infrequent changes can lead to excessive wear.

02

Wear and fatigue

Over time, camshafts can wear, especially in high-mileage vehicles.

03

Timing system issues

Faulty timing belts or chains can cause camshaft misalignment and damage.

04

Contamination

Dirty oil or debris in the engine can accelerate wear and cause camshaft issues.

Preventative maintenance

Avoiding camshaft failure

Regular maintenance and good driving habits can help prevent camshaft issues.

  • Use the correct oil specification
  • Stick to recommended service intervals
  • Address any warning lights promptly
  • Investigate unusual noises early
  • Keep the timing system in good condition
  • Use quality replacement parts
  • Avoid short journeys where possible
  • Have regular diagnostic checks
Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions answered

What are the early symptoms of camshaft failure? +
Tapping/ticking noise from top of engine, rough idle, loss of power, engine management light with cam/crank correlation codes (P0016-P0019), constant misfire, metal debris in oil.
How much does a camshaft replacement cost in the UK? +
Single cam (SOHC or one DOHC cam): £400-£900. Both cams (DOHC): £800-£1,800. Cam journal polishing: £65-£105. Complete cylinder head replacement: £1,000-£3,000+. Engine replacement: £2,500-£6,500+. Labour: 4-10 hours at £65-£95/hr indie. Engine repair cost guide
Can I still drive with a camshaft problem? +
If you have a tapping noise but no misfire, you can drive to a garage for diagnostic. If you have a misfire or cam/crank correlation codes (P0016-P0019), stop driving - especially on interference engines. Ignoring cam timing faults risks valve/piston contact and complete engine destruction.
Which engines are most prone to camshaft failure? +
Based on Engines Market enquiry volumes, BMW N47D20C (1,450 requests), Mercedes OM651 (1,250), VW/Audi EA189 (1,050), and Land Rover 204DTD (1,150) are among the most frequently requested diesel engines. Camshaft wear is one of several valve train faults specialists may investigate. Regular oil changes with correct spec oil are critical. [EM-VERIFIED]
How can I prevent camshaft failure? +
The single biggest cause of camshaft failure is oil starvation from sludge. Change the oil every 8,000-10,000 miles with manufacturer-approved spec. That £100 oil change is cheap insurance against a £1,500 camshaft repair.
Is it better to repair or replace the engine? +
Under £2,000 car value - If camshaft failed, consider scrap or used cylinder head. £2,000-£4,000 - Single cam replacement (£400-£900) viable. £4,000-£8,000 - Cam replacement or used cylinder head justified. £8,000+ - Any repair - even full cylinder head or reconditioned replacement.
