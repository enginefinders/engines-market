Crankshaft Failure UK Repair Cost, Engine Knock & Replacement

Crankshaft failure is one of the most serious engine failures. A deep knocking noise isn't a minor issue — it's the sound of your engine destroying itself from the inside. If caught early (bearing wear only), you might get away with £800–£1,500. If the crank journal is damaged or the crank snaps, engine replacement is almost always the only viable option.

Diagnostic Indicators

Don't Ignore These Warning Signs

Common symptoms of crankshaft failure or bearing wear. Early intervention is the difference between a £1,500 bearing job and an £8,500 new engine.

✓

Early Warning (Act Now)

Diagnostic stage — repair window open

  • Deep knocking noise that increases with engine RPM — not a light tick, a heavy "thud thud thud"
  • Knock is loudest under load — acceleration makes it noticeably worse
  • Oil pressure gauge reads low — or oil warning light flickers momentarily
  • Engine feels rough or vibrates excessively, especially upon cold start
⚠

When It Becomes Critical

Severe risk — stop driving immediately

  • Knock becomes constant, even at idle RPM
  • Metal flakes or bronze/copper glitter in engine oil (bearing material)
  • Sudden loss of oil pressure — gauge drops directly to zero
  • Loud bang, engine stops, oil on road — connecting rod has exited the block

Decision-Support Interface

Is Repair Economically Viable?

Whether to repair, rebuild or replace depends on the extent of damage and the host market value of your vehicle.

Repair

Bearing Replacement

  • Deep knock just started, oil pressure still normal
  • Crank journals undamaged (inspect with crank removed)
  • Vehicle value high enough to justify spend
  • Labour: 15-20 hours (engine out, sump off)
£800 – £1,500
Rebuild

Crank Machining / Regrind

  • Crank journals scored but not cracked
  • Oversized bearings available for your engine
  • Moderate damage where crank remains straight
  • Total spend economical on higher-value vehicles
£1,200 – £3,000
Replace

Complete Replacement

  • Crankshaft snapped or cracked - cannot be repaired
  • Connecting rod exited block ("windowed block")
  • Crank journal damage beyond regrind limits
  • Strip and rebuild labour exceeds replacement engine
£5,500 – £10,000+
Preventative Engineering

Avoiding Crankshaft Failure

Bearing failure is almost always caused by oil starvation or oil degradation. Follow these specialist recommendations.

01
Regular Oil Changes

Use manufacturer-spec oil (Land Rover: 5W30 C1/C2, BMW: LL04, Ford: WSS-M2C913-D).

02
Never Ignore Low Pressure

Even a momentary flicker is a red flag — stop driving and investigate immediately.

03
Listen for Cold-Start Knocks

A deep knock that disappears after 1-2 seconds is still an indicator of bearing wear.

04
Consider Oil Analysis

Detects bronze/copper bearing material before catastrophic failure on high-risk TDV6/SDV6 engines.

05
Shorter Service Intervals

Change oil every 6,000-8,000 miles instead of 15,000 miles, especially under heavy towing or motorway load.

06
Specialist Servicing

Use independent workshops familiar with oil pump housing and crankshaft tensioner weak points.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions answered regarding crankshaft failures, repairs, and replacement costs.

How much does crankshaft repair cost in the UK?
+
Bearing replacement (caught early): £800-£1,500. Crankshaft regrind / machining: £1,200-£2,000. Engine replacement (catastrophic failure): £2,700-£10,000+ depending on engine and condition. Labour: 15-25+ hours at £65-£95/hr (independent). Engine replacement cost guide
Can a snapped crankshaft be repaired?
+
No. A snapped or cracked crankshaft cannot be repaired. The only solution is a replacement engine (reconditioned, used, or rebuilt). On lower-value vehicles, this often means scrapping the car.
What causes crankshaft failure?
+
Most common causes: oil starvation (low oil level, oil pump failure), oil degradation (missed service intervals), bearing material fatigue (common on Land Rover TDV6/SDV6 from 80k-140k miles), or manufacturing defect (rare).
Which engines have the most crankshaft problems?
+
Based on 2025 quote data and KNOWN FAILURE PATTERNS: Land Rover 306DT SDV6/TDV6 (980 requests), Land Rover 276DT (700 requests), BMW N57D30 (830 requests). [EM-VERIFIED]
Is crankshaft failure covered under warranty?
+
On reconditioned engines, warranty typically covers internal component failure including crankshaft and bearings (12-24 months). Pre-existing damage or failure caused by oil starvation/oil neglect is not covered. Engines Market does not provide warranties - we are a comparison platform. Engine warranty guide
My car is worth £4,000 and has a crankshaft knock - what should I do?
+
If repair cost exceeds £2,500-£3,000, evaluate scrap value first. A reconditioned engine may cost £3,000-£5,000 fitted - often more than the car's value. Consider a used engine (£1,500-£2,500 fitted) if the car is otherwise sound, or scrap the vehicle and replace it.
