Symptoms

Signs Your Automatic Gearbox Needs Attention

Automatic gearboxes give you warning signs - but they're different from manuals. Don't ignore them.

Harsh or jerky shifting
Valve body issue, worn clutch packs, low fluid
⚠️ Get checked - progressive wear
Slipping between gears
Worn clutch packs, low fluid pressure, fluid contamination
⚠️ Get checked - could lead to complete failure
Delayed engagement (D or R)
Low fluid, worn pump, solenoid issue, valve body wear
⚠️ Get checked - early indicator of serious issues
Transmission overheating
Low fluid, clogged cooler, internal wear
🔴 Get checked immediately - heat destroys automatics
Transmission warning light
Internal fault - fluid temp, solenoid, sensor, speed sensor
🔴 Get checked - driving could cause catastrophic damage
Whining or humming noise
Worn bearings, low fluid, pump wear
⚠️ Get checked - progressive wear
Burning smell
Overheated fluid - clutch packs slipping
🔴 Stop driving - possible fire risk
Leaking fluid (red/brown)
Seals worn, damaged casing, cooler line leak
⚠️ Get checked - low fluid causes internal wear
Rough idle in gear
Torque converter issue, engine mount wear
⚠️ Get checked - could be converter or mounts
No drive in any gear
Complete internal failure, pump failure, valve body failure
🔴 Do not drive - immediate replacement needed

When to Stop Driving Immediately

Complete loss of drive Major internal failure - driving is impossible.
Transmission warning light Internal fault - driving could destroy the gearbox.
Burning smell Fluid overheating - clutch packs are slipping.
Loud grinding / clunking Internal damage - driving could cause catastrophic failure.

Valve body note

The Valve Body / Mechatronic Note - Not Always Full Replacement

Many automatic gearbox issues are valve body or mechatronic unit failures - and these can often be repaired rather than replaced.

🔧 What Is the Valve Body / Mechatronic Unit?
  • Valve Body - Controls fluid flow to clutch packs. Like the "brain" of the gearbox. Failure signs: harsh shifting, delayed engagement, slipping.
  • Mechatronic Unit - Integrated valve body + electronics (common in DSG/DCT). Failure signs: same as above + warning lights, limp mode.
  • Solenoids - Electrically controlled valves within the valve body. Failure signs: individual gear issues, delayed engagement.

"About 40% of automatic gearbox 'failures' we see are actually valve body or mechatronic issues - not full gearbox failures. The customer thinks they need a new gearbox, but a £500-£1,000 valve body repair solves the problem. Always get a proper diagnostic before assuming the worst."

Decision framework

Repair vs Replacement Decision Framework

Use this framework to decide whether to repair or replace your automatic gearbox.

⚠️ Harsh shifting, no slipping, no warning light
Repair - likely valve body issue (£400-£1,000)
⚠️ Delayed engagement, no slipping
Repair - likely valve body or solenoid (£400-£1,200)
❌ Slipping between gears, warning light on
Replace - clutch packs worn - internal damage likely
❌ Burning smell, overheating
Replace - fluid contamination - internal damage
❌ Complete loss of drive
Replace - major internal failure
✅ Warning light only - no drivability issues
Diagnose first - could be sensor (£100-£300)
❌ Vehicle value under £3,000, replacement over £2,000
Scrap or used - replacement is uneconomical

The Threshold Rule

Under 30% ✅ Repair - financially sound
30-50% ⚠️ Evaluate carefully - consider vehicle value
Over 50% ❌ Replace - repair is approaching replacement cost

Frequently asked questions

Automatic Gearbox FAQs

How much does automatic gearbox replacement cost? −
Torque converter automatic £1,000-£4,000, CVT £1,200-£5,000, DSG/DCT £1,500-£7,000. Prices vary by gearbox type, condition, and vehicle.
What's the difference between torque converter, CVT, and DSG? +
Torque converter - traditional auto, smooth, uses fluid coupling. CVT - uses pulleys and belts, seamless ratio changes. DSG/DCT - dual-clutch, fast shifts, sporty.
Can a valve body issue be repaired without replacing the whole gearbox? +
Yes - many automatic gearbox issues (harsh shifting, delayed engagement) are valve body or mechatronic issues. Repair costs £400-£1,200 - far less than full replacement.
What are the signs of automatic gearbox failure? +
Harsh shifting, slipping between gears, delayed engagement, transmission overheating, transmission warning light, whining noise, burning smell, leaking fluid.
How long does automatic gearbox replacement take? +
Labour typically takes 8-15 hours (1-3 days). Additional time for sourcing the gearbox. Allow 3-7 days for the complete process.
What warranty comes with an automatic gearbox replacement? +
Used: 3-6 months. Reconditioned: 6-24 months. New: 12-24 months. Always check the specific terms with your supplier.
Is automatic gearbox replacement more expensive than manual? +
Yes - automatic gearboxes are more complex, have more components, and require more labour. Typical automatic replacement costs £1,000-£5,000 vs manual £500-£3,000.
Do I need to tell the DVLA and my insurer? +
Gearbox replacement doesn't require DVLA notification. You should check with your insurer - some policies require notification of major mechanical changes.
