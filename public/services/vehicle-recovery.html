Why Flatbed

Flatbed Recovery - Why It's the Right Choice

Not all recovery methods are equal. Here's why flatbed is the only safe option for engine and gearbox failures.

🛻 Flatbed Recovery
✅ Safe - Minimal risk to vehicle
Vehicle fully secured - all wheels off the ground
Best for: All vehicles - especially those with engine/gearbox failure
🔗 Tow Rope
❌ Not safe - High risk
Can damage suspension, gearbox, bodywork
Best for: Emergency only - not recommended
🔧 Tow Bar / A-Frame
⚠️ Partial - Medium risk
Can damage gearbox in automatics
Best for: Only if vehicle is drivable and follows regulations
🛞 Dolly Towing
⚠️ Partial - Medium risk
Can damage gearbox
Best for: Specialist only
No gearbox damage

All wheels are off the ground - no strain on transmission.

No suspension damage

Vehicle is fully supported - no bouncing or stress.

Safe for automatics

No risk of transmission damage from towing.

Safe for low-profile vehicles

No risk of scraping or bottoming out.

Professional loading

Vehicle is properly secured - no movement during transport.

Checklist

Recovery Checklist - What to Do Before Collection

A step-by-step guide to prepare your vehicle for recovery.

Before the Recovery Vehicle Arrives

1
Remove personal items
Empty the vehicle of all personal belongings - they may be lost or damaged during transport.
2
Remove toll passes / tickets
Remove any parking permits, toll tags, or passes - they may not be returned.
3
Check vehicle is clear
Ensure the vehicle is accessible - clear any obstructions for safe loading.
4
Release handbrake
If safe to do so - for loading purposes, the driver may need to move the vehicle.
5
Note fuel level
Record the current fuel level for your records - in case the workshop needs to move it.
6
Check for damage
Walk around the vehicle - take photographs of any existing damage for your records.
7
Photograph the vehicle
Take clear photos of all four sides and the interior - evidence of condition before transport.
8
Have keys ready
Have all keys ready for the driver - they need to move the vehicle onto the flatbed.

During the Collection

1
Ask for the driver's ID
Ask for the driver's ID or company identification - verify they are from the recovery company you booked.
2
Ask about any images taken
Ask the driver if they have taken photos of the vehicle for their records.
3
Observe the loading
Watch the vehicle being loaded onto the flatbed - ensure it is loaded safely and properly secured.
4
Confirm the destination
Confirm the workshop address with the driver - avoid any confusion about the destination.
5
Get a reference number
Ask for a job reference number or tracking number - so you can track the vehicle if needed.
6
Take photos during loading
Take photos of the vehicle being loaded - additional evidence of condition during transport.

After Collection

1
Confirm arrival time
Confirm the expected arrival time at the workshop - so you can notify the workshop.
2
Keep the reference number
Keep the job reference number handy - for tracking and any follow-up.
3
Contact the workshop
Confirm the workshop is ready to receive the vehicle - avoid any delays at the drop-off.
Supplier Checklist

What to Ask the Recovery Supplier Before Booking

A checklist of questions to protect yourself and avoid surprises.

What type of recovery vehicle will you use?

Confirm it's a flatbed - not a tow rope or A-frame.

What's the estimated arrival time?

Manage your expectations - how long will you wait?

What's the total cost - any additional charges?

Check for hidden charges - storage, mileage overrun, etc.

What's the cancellation policy?

If your plans change, can you cancel without penalty?

Do you have insurance for transport damage?

Confirm they have insurance - what's the claims process?

Can I get a tracking number?

So you can track the vehicle during transport.

Do you take photos of the vehicle before loading?

Many recovery companies do - this protects both parties.

What happens if the workshop isn't ready?

Is there a storage charge? What's the process?

What should I do if there's an issue?

Contact process - who do you speak to?

Don't Assume

What NOT to Ask or Assume

Common misconceptions that can lead to problems.

❌ "Engines Market arranges the recovery"

No - we connect you with recovery specialists. You arrange directly with the recovery supplier.

❌ "The recovery driver can fix my car"

No - they are transport specialists, not mechanics. Recovery transports - repairs are separate.

⚠️ "My breakdown cover will cover this"

Maybe - check your policy. Some cover recovery to a garage, some don't.

❌ "The driver will take the vehicle to a workshop of their choice"

No - you choose the workshop. Confirm the destination with the driver.

❌ "I don't need to take photos - it's all fine"

Wrong - always take photos. Photograph the vehicle before, during, and after transport.

What type of recovery should I choose for engine failure? +
Flatbed recovery is the only safe option for engine and gearbox failures. Tow ropes can damage the vehicle - especially automatics.
Should I take photos before recovery? +
Yes - always take clear photos of all four sides and the interior before the vehicle is collected. Also ask the recovery driver if they have taken photos. This protects you in case of transport damage.

Some recovery companies charge storage fees if the workshop isn't ready. Always confirm the workshop is ready before booking recovery.
