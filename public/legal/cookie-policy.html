Introduction

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help us make the Platform work, remember your choices, and understand how visitors interact with our pages.

Cookies are not viruses or spyware. They cannot access your hard drive or personal information that you haven't already provided. Most cookies are session cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (remain for a set period).

We also use similar technologies such as local storage and pixel tags — but for simplicity, we refer to all of them as “cookies” in this policy.

If we ever introduce advertising cookies in the future, we will update this policy, notify you via our cookie banner, and require explicit consent before placing any such cookies.

User Controls

Consent and how to manage cookies

4.1 Our cookie consent banner

When you first visit our Platform, a cookie banner will appear. The banner allows you to:

  • Accept all cookies (strictly necessary + performance + functional — we do not have targeting cookies).
  • Reject non-essential cookies (accept only strictly necessary).
  • Customise your preferences (choose which categories to accept).

Your preference is stored in the cookie_consent cookie. You can change your preference at any time by clicking the “Cookie Preferences” link in the footer.

4.2 Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw your consent to non-essential cookies at any time by:

  • Clicking “Cookie Preferences” in the footer and adjusting your settings.
  • Clearing your browser cookies (this will reset your preference).
  • Changing your browser settings to block cookies (see section 4.3 below).

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

4.3 Managing cookies via your browser

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Block all cookies (including strictly necessary ones) — but note that our Platform may not function properly.
  • Block third-party cookies only.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Set your browser to alert you when a cookie is placed.
Browser How to manage cookies
Google Chrome Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
Mozilla Firefox Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
Apple Safari Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
Microsoft Edge Settings → Site permissions → Cookies and site data
Opera Settings → Privacy & security → Cookies

You can also use the Your Online Choices tool to opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies.

Governance & Retention

Duration, changes & further information

How long do cookies stay on my device?

The duration varies by cookie (see the “Duration” column in the tables above):

  • Session cookies — deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies — remain for a set period (e.g., 30 days, 12 months, 2 years) unless you delete them manually.

You can delete all cookies stored on your device at any time via your browser settings.

Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use, legal requirements, or our business practices.

  • Minor changes (e.g., adding a new strictly necessary cookie) will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
  • Material changes (e.g., introducing targeting/advertising cookies) will be notified via a prominent notice on our Platform and, where appropriate, by email. We will also refresh the cookie consent banner to obtain fresh consent where required by law.

The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised. You are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
