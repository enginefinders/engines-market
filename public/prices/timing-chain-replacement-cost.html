The short answer

What Does Timing Chain Replacement Cost in the UK?

Timing chain replacement costs range from £750 to £2,700+ depending on your vehicle make, engine type, and location. BMW N47 and Land Rover Ingenium engines are at the higher end (£1,500-£2,700), while Ford and Vauxhall are typically lower (£750-£1,500). Early replacement saves thousands compared to a snapped chain (£4,000+ for engine replacement).

For most UK drivers: Timing chain replacement is a significant but necessary expense. Here's what you can expect by vehicle type:
  • Small car (Fiesta, Corsa): £750-£1,500
  • Family car (Focus, Golf, A3): £900-£1,800
  • Executive (BMW 3 Series, Audi A4, Mercedes C-Class): £1,200-£2,500
  • SUV (Range Rover Evoque, Discovery Sport): £1,500-£2,700
  • Large SUV (Range Rover Sport, Audi Q7, BMW X5): £1,500-£3,000+

Why timing chain replacement is more expensive
  • Labour time is significantly higher - 8-20 hours vs 3-6 hours for a belt
  • Access is more difficult - often at the rear of the engine (BMW N47)
  • More components to replace - chain, guides, tensioners, sprockets, sometimes VVT units
  • Specialist tools required - camshaft locking tools, timing tools
  • Specialist knowledge required - mis-timing the engine destroys it
Why timing belts are cheaper
  • Labour time is lower - 3-6 hours for most vehicles
  • Access is easier - usually at the front of the engine
  • Fewer components to replace - belt, tensioner, water pump (often)
  • Less specialist knowledge required - still requires correct timing but less complex

What's included - and what's not

Know exactly what you're paying for

Clarity matters. Below is a typical breakdown for a timing chain replacement at an independent specialist.

✅ Typically included

🔗 Timing chain kit
Chain, guides, tensioners, sprockets (sometimes VVT units). Parts cost £300-£800.
🛠️ Gaskets and seals
Timing cover gasket, valve cover gasket, crankshaft seal. £50-£200.
🔄 Oil and filter change
Fresh engine oil and filter after replacement. £50-£150.
🔧 Labour
Removal of components, chain replacement, timing setting. £500-£2,000+.
📋 Testing
Running the engine, checking for correct timing. Included in labour.
🗑️ Disposal
Old parts disposal. Included.

⚠️ Typically NOT included (extra cost)

⚙️ VVT units
Sometimes need replacing if worn. Extra £200-£600 each.
🔩 Camshafts
If worn from chain failure. Extra £300-£800 each.
🚪 Valves
If engine has been run with timing issues. Extra £100-£300 each.
🏗️ Cylinder head
If valves have bent. Extra £500-£2,000.
🚗 Engine replacement
If chain snaps and destroys engine. Extra £4,000+.

Always ask your supplier for a full written breakdown before agreeing to any work. Additional costs are the biggest source of disputes.

Before you agree

10 questions to ask before replacing your timing chain

Asking the right questions can save you thousands and prevent disputes. Here's a checklist specific to timing chain replacement.

1. "What's included in the timing chain kit?"

Ask specifically about: chain(s), guides, tensioners, sprockets, VVT units, and gaskets.

2. "Is the chain at the front or rear of the engine?"

This determines how much labour is required. Rear chain jobs take significantly longer and cost more.

3. "Do you need to remove the engine or gearbox?"

For some engines (BMW N47/N57), the engine or gearbox must be removed. This adds labour and cost.

4. "What warranty do you offer on the replacement?"

Typical warranty for timing chain work is 12-24 months on parts and labour. Get it in writing.

5. "What additional work is recommended while the engine is apart?"

Ask if VVT units, water pump, or other components should be replaced. Doing it now saves labour later.

6. "What's the total cost including all parts and labour?"

Get a full written breakdown. Ask about any potential additional costs.

7. "What happens if additional issues are discovered during replacement?"

During replacement, other issues may be discovered (worn guides, damaged components). Ask how these are handled.

8. "What's the timeline from booking to completion?"

Get a realistic estimate. Front chain typically 1-2 days; rear chain 2-3 days.

9. "Do you have experience with my engine type?"

Ask about the specialist's experience with your specific engine. BMW N47 and Land Rover Ingenium require specialist knowledge.

10. "Can you provide a written quote?"

Get everything in writing before agreeing. The scope of work, cost, timeline, and warranty terms should all be documented.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about timing chain replacement

How much does timing chain replacement cost in the UK? +
Timing chain replacement costs range from £750 to £2,700+ depending on vehicle make and engine type. BMW N47 and Land Rover Ingenium are at the higher end (£1,500-£2,700), while Ford and Vauxhall are lower (£750-£1,500). Early replacement saves thousands compared to engine replacement (£4,000+).
How do I know if my timing chain is failing? +
Look for these signs: rattling on startup, rattling when warm, engine management light (cam/crank correlation faults), loss of power, poor fuel economy, rough idle, or difficult starting. Rattling on startup is one of the earliest warning signs - don't ignore it.
What happens if my timing chain snaps? +
Catastrophic engine failure. On interference engines (most modern petrol and diesel engines), the pistons hit the valves. Valves bend, pistons crack, and the cylinder head is damaged. The engine is destroyed. Engine replacement costs £4,000+.
How long does timing chain replacement take? +
Typical replacement time is 1-3 days. Front chain (most engines) takes 1-2 days. Rear chain (BMW N47/N57) takes 2-3 days. V6/V8 engines take longer. The timeline depends on vehicle complexity and parts availability.
What's the difference between a timing chain and timing belt? +
A timing chain is metal and lasts longer (100,000+ miles) but costs more to replace (£750-£2,700). A timing belt is rubber and must be replaced every 40,000-80,000 miles but is cheaper (£300-£700). Labour for a chain is also higher.
Is timing chain replacement covered by warranty? +
If the vehicle is under manufacturer warranty (usually 3-5 years), timing chain failure should be covered. If the vehicle is outside warranty, you'll need to pay for replacement. Extended warranties may cover timing chain failure - check your policy.
Which vehicles are most at risk of timing chain failure? +
BMW N47 (2007-2015), Land Rover Ingenium (2015-2020), BMW N57 (2008-2016), BMW N20 (2011-2016), Mercedes OM651 (2008-2016), and Ford 1.0 EcoBoost (2012-2020). These engines have known timing chain issues. See the Red Flag Vehicles table above for full details.
Can I drive with a rattling timing chain? +
No - you should stop driving immediately. A rattling timing chain means the chain or tensioner is failing. Continuing to drive risks the chain snapping, which destroys the engine. Have it inspected immediately.
Should I replace the timing chain preventatively? +
If you own a high-risk vehicle (BMW N47, Land Rover Ingenium), preventative replacement is strongly recommended. Replacing the chain before it fails costs £1,500-£2,700. If it snaps, engine replacement costs £4,000+.
Why does timing chain replacement cost more on BMW and Land Rover? +
BMW N47/N57 have the chain at the rear of the engine - the gearbox or engine must be removed. This significantly increases labour time (12-18 hours vs 8-12 hours). Land Rover Ingenium also has high labour requirements and specialist knowledge.
What's included in a timing chain replacement? +
Timing chain kit (chain, guides, tensioners, sprockets), gaskets and seals, oil and filter change, labour, and testing. Some kits include VVT units - check with your specialist. See the "What's included" section above for full details.
Is it worth replacing the timing chain on an older car? +
If the car is otherwise in good condition and worth £3,000+, it's usually worth replacing the chain. If the car is worth under £2,000, consider whether replacement is economically viable. However, if the chain snaps, you'll need a new engine (£4,000+) or a new car.
How often should a timing chain be replaced? +
Most manufacturers describe timing chains as "lifetime" components. In practice, chains typically last 100,000-150,000 miles. However, some engines (BMW N47, Land Rover Ingenium) can fail at 50,000-80,000 miles. Regular oil changes and listening for rattling are essential.
Do I need to tell my insurer about a new timing chain? +
Yes. Any major mechanical work, including timing chain replacement, should be declared to your insurer. The work itself may affect vehicle value and should be recorded.
