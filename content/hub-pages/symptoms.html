Identify Your Engine Problem

Engine problems often start with a noise, a warning light, or a change in performance. This hub organises symptoms by type, helping you identify what's wrong before you visit a garage. Once you know the symptom, we'll guide you to the likely causes and repair costs.

Engine Noises

3 symptoms
Rattling Noise Check Soon
Metallic rattle, especially on cold start
Likely causes: Timing chain wear, heat shield, exhaust
Repair cost: £50–£10,000+
Knocking Noise Stop Immediately
Deep, rhythmic knock from engine
Likely causes: Rod bearing failure, detonation
Repair cost: £150–£10,000+
Ticking Noise Monitor
Rapid tapping from engine
Likely causes: Valve train, low oil, injectors
Repair cost: £60–£2,000

Exhaust Smoke

3 symptoms
White Smoke Stop Immediately
Thick white smoke from exhaust
Likely causes: Head gasket failure (coolant burning)
Repair cost: £550–£2,000+
Blue Smoke Check Soon
Blue / grey smoke from exhaust
Likely causes: Oil burning — rings, seals, turbo
Repair cost: £100–£4,000+
Black Smoke Check Soon
Black / dark smoke from exhaust
Likely causes: Over-fuelling — injectors, MAF
Repair cost: £100–£900

Performance Issues

5 symptoms
Loss of Power Check Soon
Car lacks acceleration
Likely causes: Turbo, fuel, timing, compression
Repair cost: £20–£10,000+
Engine Won't Start Call for Help
Engine cranks but won't fire
Likely causes: Fuel pump, spark, battery, seizure
Repair cost: £60–£8,000+
Rough Idle Check Soon
Engine shakes at idle
Likely causes: Misfire, vacuum leak, injectors
Repair cost: £60–£900
Engine Stalling Check Soon
Engine dies while driving
Likely causes: Fuel pump, sensor, ECU
Repair cost: £50–£1,200
Limp Mode Check Soon
Reduced speed / rev limit
Likely causes: DPF, turbo, sensor failure
Repair cost: £45–£3,500+

Fluid & Warning Issues

3 symptoms
Excessive Oil Use Monitor
Frequent oil top-ups
Likely causes: Rings, seals, PCV, turbo
Repair cost: £100–£4,000+
Coolant Loss Check Soon
Losing coolant between services
Likely causes: Leak, head gasket, water pump
Repair cost: £5–£2,000+
Metal Shavings in Oil Stop Immediately
Glitter or flakes in oil
Likely causes: Bearing wear, cylinder damage
Repair cost: £800–£10,000+

Dashboard Warning Lights

4 symptoms
Oil Warning Light Stop Immediately
Red oil can on dashboard
Likely causes: Low oil, oil pump failure
Repair cost: £60–£10,000+
Coolant Warning Light Stop Immediately
Temperature or level warning
Likely causes: Low coolant, overheating
Repair cost: £5–£5,000+
DPF Warning Light Check Soon
DPF symbol on dashboard
Likely causes: Blocked DPF — short journeys
Repair cost: £85–£3,500+
Check Engine Light Diagnose Soon
Engine management light
Likely causes: Various — OBD2 code needed
Repair cost: £0–£2,500+

When to Stop Driving Immediately

If you experience any of these symptoms, pull over safely and call for help.
Red oil pressure warning Stop immediately
Temperature gauge in red zone Stop immediately
Knocking noise (deep, rhythmic) Stop immediately
Engine management light flashing Stop immediately
Thick white smoke Stop immediately
