What These Case Studies Teach Us

The £4,000–£8,000 Rule

In most cases, a dealer quote exceeds the vehicle's value. Specialist reconditioned engines saved owners 40–70% compared to dealer quotes — often making an uneconomical repair viable.

The Importance of Early Diagnosis

Timing chain and head gasket failures caught early cost £800–£1,800. Ignored, they cost £3,500–£10,000+. Early diagnosis is the single biggest cost-saving factor.

The 6,000-Mile Oil Change Rule

Engines that failed prematurely (N47, SDV6, M9R, EcoBoost) often had extended service intervals. 6,000–8,000-mile oil changes significantly reduce failure risk.
