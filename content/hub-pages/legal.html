Who We Are & What We Stand For

Engines Market is a UK engine price comparison and marketplace platform. Our purpose is to help vehicle owners compare replacement engine options from independent UK suppliers.

About Engines Market

We provide pricing transparency, market intelligence, educational guidance, and supplier introductions. Our mission is to make engine replacement pricing transparent across the UK.

We are a comparison platform, not a contracting party.

We do not supply engines ourselves We do not repair vehicles We do not fit engines We do not process customer payments We do not provide warranties We connect you with vetted specialists
