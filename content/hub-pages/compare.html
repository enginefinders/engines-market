The Vehicle Value Rule

Use your vehicle's current market value to guide your decision.
Vehicle Value Best Option Reasoning
Under £3,000 Scrap or used engine Replacement cost exceeds vehicle value
£3,000–£5,000 Used engine Budget option minimises loss
£5,000–£8,000 Evaluate both Reconditioned may be viable
£8,000–£15,000 Reconditioned Warranty justifies premium
£15,000+ Reconditioned or rebuilt Premium option recommended

The Ownership Horizon Rule

How long you plan to keep the vehicle determines the best option.
Ownership Period Best Option Reasoning
Under 1 year Used engine Minimise upfront cost
1–2 years Used or reconditioned Balance cost and reliability
2–5 years Reconditioned Warranty covers ownership period
5+ years Rebuilt or new Long-term reliability
